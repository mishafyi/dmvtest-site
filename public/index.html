DMV Prep: California

Practice for the California DMV written test in the language you think in, with the English wording beside it.

On the App Store as “CA DMV Practice Test 2027”. iPhone and iPad.

Built for the California test

  • 1000+ questions, written from the official California handbooks, in the real exam format: 36 questions with 6 mistakes allowed at 18 or older, 46 with 8 allowed under 18. 471 cover the driver's license (Class C) and 546 the motorcycle license (Class M).
  • Eleven languages, side by side. English · Español · 中文 · Tiếng Việt · 한국어 · Русский · العربية · فارسی · Armenian · Punjabi · Tagalog. Every question, answer and explanation is translated for both licenses, and the English sits under each question, so you learn the examiner's words too.
  • A guided path, not an endless list. 35 short lessons for each license, each pairing theory cards in the handbook's own words with a practice round worth up to three stars. The Permit Test Simulator ends the path, and spaced repetition brings back what you missed.
  • The handbook as real text, searchable and offline, all 41 road signs with their meanings, and an AI tutor that runs entirely on an iPhone with Apple Intelligence.
  • Private and offline. No account, no ads, no tracking. Everything except the official DMV links works with no connection, and your progress follows you between iPhone and iPad through your own iCloud.

Road sign artwork is from the FHWA MUTCD (public domain). The official California Driver's Handbook is free at dmv.ca.gov.
