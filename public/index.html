DMV Prep: California

Practice for the California DMV written test in the language you think in, with the English wording beside it.

On the App Store as “DMV Written Test Prep - 2026”. iPhone and iPad.

Built for the California test

  • 962 questions per language, written from the official California Driver's Handbook, in the real exam format: 36 questions with 6 mistakes allowed at 18 or older, 46 with 8 allowed under 18. A separate set covers the Motorcycle (Class M) test.
  • Eleven languages, side by side. English · Español · 中文 · Tiếng Việt · 한국어 · Русский · العربية · فارسی · Armenian · Punjabi · Tagalog. Tap any question to see the English underneath.
  • A guided path, not an endless list. 71 lessons of 10 questions in six steps, an exam simulator at the end, and spaced repetition that brings back what you missed.
  • The handbook as real text, searchable and offline, plus an AI tutor that runs entirely on an iPhone with Apple Intelligence.
  • Private and offline. No account, no ads, no tracking. Nothing leaves your phone.
